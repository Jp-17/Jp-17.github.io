About

I'm a PhD student in Computational Neuroscience at Tsinghua University, Beijing, supervised by Prof. Xiaoxuan Jia in the Neural Coding Lab. My work sits at the intersection of neuroscience and AI, to understand how intelligent systems (brains and models) represent, reuse, and generalize knowledge.

Currently I am working on Neural Foundation Model research, to build large-scale pre-trained models for neural spiking data, which can generalize across brain areas, sessions, and subjects. The goal is to understand how information is represented and encoded in the brain across different modalities, and how neural activity can be decoded into behaviors and visual experiences such as images and videos.

Previously, I explored startup opportunities in the area of real-time interactive digital humans.
